## 2. Inviolable Governance & Offline Fallback Seams

In accordance with `DAO.md`:
- **Truth in Evidence**: Local procedural generations (`ProceduralIllustrator`) are clearly identified with provider `local`. AI-generated assets are labeled with provider `meta` and model identifier `muse-image-1.0` or `muse-wonder-1.0`.
- **Zero Default Leakage**: When operating without a `META_API_KEY`, all story retelling, audio extraction (24kHz RMS, dynamic range, pause ratio, WPM), and dialect preservation remain 100% in-browser.
- **Privacy & Child Data**: No children's voice recordings or private journal entries are uploaded without explicit user configuration.
