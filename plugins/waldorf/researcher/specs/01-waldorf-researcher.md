# Waldorf Pedagogical & Cultural Research Resources

> Focuses exclusively on Waldorf education, 3 painting techniques, Vietnamese folklore, and regional dialect preservation.

## Research Verdicts

1. **Goethean Color Theory for Wet-on-Wet Watercolor**: `ADOPT`
   - Using warm yellow, vermilion rose, and ultramarine blue to evoke emotional qualities suitable for young children.
2. **Vietnamese Regional Dialect Preservation**: `ADOPT`
   - Preserving authentic phonemes across Quảng Nam, Central, Northern, and Southern regions rather than standardizing speech.
3. **Local Storytelling Motifs**: `ADOPT`
   - Connecting stories to Vietnamese agricultural life (monsoon seasons, river estuaries, traditional crafts like Thanh Hà pottery and Kim Bồng carpentry).
