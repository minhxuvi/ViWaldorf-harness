# 01 — Waldorf Pedagogy & Three Art Techniques

> Governed by [DAO.md](../../../DAO.md).

## 1. Pedagogical Foundation

Waldorf education honors the threefold nature of the growing child:
- **Thinking (Head)**: Clear, imaginative understanding born from living experience.
- **Feeling (Heart)**: Aesthetic, empathetic connection cultivated through storytelling, poetry, and song.
- **Willing (Hands)**: Purposeful, constructive action through practical crafts and artistic work.

## 2. Three Waldorf Art Techniques

### 1. Wet-on-Wet Watercolor (`wet-on-wet`)
- **Recommended Age**: Kindergarten & Grades 1–3 (ages 3–9).
- **Technique**: Natural mineral/plant watercolors applied to thoroughly moistened heavy cotton paper.
- **Color Theory**: Grounded in Goethe's *Theory of Colours*—exploring luminous polarities of warm lemon yellow, vermilion red, and ultramarine blue.
- **Visual Character**: Soft, breathing edges, fluid color interpenetration, zero hard black ink outlines.

### 2. Beeswax Block Crayon (`crayon`)
- **Recommended Age**: Grades 1–5 (ages 6–11).
- **Technique**: Flat rectangular blocks of pure beeswax infused with natural pigments, applied on warm cream textured paper.
- **Visual Character**: Broad sweeping diagonal strokes, organic warm earth tones (ochre, terracotta, moss green), layered translucent wax textures.

### 3. Chalkboard Drawing (`chalkboard`)
- **Recommended Age**: Grades 1–8 (ages 6–14).
- **Technique**: Multilayered soft pastel chalks on dark matte slate blackboards (Waldorf Teacher Main Lesson Drawing).
- **Visual Character**: Radiant light emerging from dark background, atmospheric depth, golden highlights, no text or mechanical grids.
