# 00. Master Prompt: ViWaldorf Pure Storytelling Practice

## 1. Intent & Outcome
- **ViWaldorf Storytelling** is a focused, offline-first pedagogical practice tool designed exclusively to help parents and educators internalize and tell cultural stories aloud in Vietnam.
- **Five Progressive Practice Levels (Level 0 – Level 4)**:
  1. **Level 0 — Không gian bối cảnh & Linh hồn câu chuyện**: Grasp the cultural spirit, storytelling rhythm, age-appropriate metaphorical meaning, and ancient/regional pronunciation guides before reading the full text.
  2. **Level 1 — Toàn văn câu chuyện**: Complete read-aloud narrative rich in sensory imagery, paired with explicit *Lưu ý giọng kể* (vocal delivery notes) for each scene.
  3. **Level 2 — Dàn ý chi tiết**: Practice retelling without the full text while retaining all characters, localized details, and age-appropriate emotional rhythm.
  4. **Level 3 — Dàn ý tóm tắt**: One single unified skeleton outline closely following the original plot (4–6 concise core points per scene).
  5. **Level 4 — Tranh gợi nhớ thuần hình ảnh**: One traditional hand-drawn Waldorf illustration per scene (**Wet-on-Wet Watercolor**, **Beeswax Crayon**, or **Pastel Chalkboard**) rendered purely visually with **zero text hints**, empowering the storyteller's living spoken language.
- **Pure Voice Recording & Playback**:
  - Direct in-browser voice recording (`MediaRecorder`) and playback (`<audio controls>`) so the storyteller can listen back to their own voice naturally.

## 2. Hard Constraints & Non-Goals
- **Zero AI Features**: No remote AI APIs, no AI chat, no automated story scoring, no acoustic/transcript grading, and no speech-to-text evaluation.
- **Single-Focus Scope**: No weekly timetables, classroom curriculum documents, student rosters, or cloud sync infrastructure.
- **Privacy First**: Audio recordings remain strictly in-memory / on the user's device; zero network transmission.
