# 00. Master Prompt: A Product for Waldorf-Inspired Education Approach in Vietnam

## 1. Intent & Outcome
- The product provides **ViWaldorf Story**: a pedagogical suite that helps parents and educators practice telling cultural folktales:
  1. Upload or paste a story, automatically divide it into memorable scenes.
  2. Generate age-appropriate illustrations using three authentic Waldorf artistic techniques:
     - **Wet-on-Wet Watercolor** (Goethean color theory, soft diffused washes)
     - **Crayon Drawing** (broad textured strokes with natural beeswax crayons on warm paper)
     - **Chalkboard Drawing** (luminous pastel chalks on dark slate)
  3. Record oral retellings, measure acoustic delivery directly in the browser (duration, RMS dB loudness, dynamic range, pause ratio %, speaking pace WPM).
  4. Edit transcriptions, preserve regional Vietnamese dialects without flattening accents, and receive encouraging, holistic feedback.
- Provide live generation, validation, and Markdown export for 6 mandatory curriculum documents:
  1. Overall Educational Program
  2. Grade-Level Program
  3. Alignment Matrix with General Education Curriculum (GDPT)
  4. Academic Year Education Plan
  5. Weekly Timetable & Rhythm Plan (08:00–15:00 Monday–Friday)
  6. Teaching Journal & Observational Student Assessment Records

## 2. Hard Constraints & Non-Goals
- Privacy first: no child data transmission without explicit consent.
- Honest attribution: distinguish local procedural algorithms from AI models.
- No punitive numerical grades or ranking for children.
