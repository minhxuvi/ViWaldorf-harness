# 03 — Six Classroom Educational Documents

> Governed by [DAO.md](../../../DAO.md).

The system generates 6 comprehensive curriculum documents synthesized from current class data:

## 1. Overall Educational Program
- Educational philosophy, developmental worldview, core qualities, learning competencies, main lesson structures, assessment guidelines.

## 2. Grade-Level Program
- Grade-specific themes (e.g. Grade 3: Farming, House Building, Money & Trade, Old Testament stories), learning outcomes, instructional hours, materials.

## 3. Alignment Matrix with General Education Curriculum (GDPT)
- Cross-walk showing how experiential Waldorf lessons satisfy official national curriculum standards without sacrificing aesthetic depth.

## 4. Academic Year Education Plan
- 35 school weeks divided into 3-4 week Main Lesson blocks, monthly themes, seasonal festivals (Mid-Autumn, Lantern Walk, Winter Solstice, Spring Tet).

## 5. Weekly Timetable & Rhythm Plan
- Detailed Monday–Friday 08:00–15:00 schedule distinguishing Instructional time (Main lesson, Languages, Arts, Handwork) from Living Rhythm (Snack, Free play, Outdoor exploration).

## 6. Teaching Journal & Observational Assessment Records
- Teacher's daily observation notes, student progress reflections, supportive accommodations (no punitive letter or numeric grades).
