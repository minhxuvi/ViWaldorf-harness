# 02 — Recording Template and Benchmark History

> Governed by [DAO.md](../../DAO.md).

## Benchmark Record Template

Every benchmark measurement must record:
1. Date and cycle identifier.
2. Veto gate outcomes (`G1`, `G2`, `G3`).
3. Scores across `B1` through `B8` with evidence levels (`live`, `automated`, `manual`, `mock`, `unverified`).
4. Autonomous verdict (`KEEP`, `PARK`, `REVERT`).
